# Domain modeling

Owner: Documentation

**Status:** Not yet formally defined.

Breeze On does not currently maintain organization-wide standards for business or domain modeling.

## Scope of future standards

When conventions are established, this document is expected to cover areas such as:

- Business entities and naming
- Process models
- Workflow representations
- DTO conventions
- Domain boundaries

## Current practice

Until organization-wide standards exist, repositories may document and apply their own modeling conventions. Do not treat local patterns in one repo as org standards unless they are written here.

## For contributors

When modeling conventions become agreed and implemented across the organization, update this document to describe **current reality only**. Do not invent modeling rules ahead of adoption.
